# ImagineClip: AI Video Generator for Social Clips

## Summary
ImagineClip is an AI video generator for creating social-ready clips, avatar videos, stylized scenes, and visual effects from prompts and images. New users receive 50 free credits after registration.

![ImagineClip preview](https://imagineclip.com/og.png)

## Key Points
- AI video generation from prompts and images.
- Avatar videos, stylized scenes, and shareable social clips.
- Free signup credits for new users.
